Intent & Privacy Statement Welcome. This space was created as an evolving portfolio, research project, and creative environment. Visitors may be invited to share reflections, thoughts, or reasons for visiting. Why I Ask Responses help me understand how people discover, navigate, and experience this space. They inform future design decisions and help me learn about the audiences engaging with my work. What May Be Collected If you choose to participate, I may store: - Your response to visitor questions - The date and time of your visit - Technical information necessary for the website to function Please avoid sharing sensitive, confidential, or personally identifying information. How Responses Are Used Responses are reviewed for research, reflection, and portfolio development purposes. Individual responses will not be published in a way that identifies you without your permission. Your Choice Participation is voluntary. If a question does not feel relevant or comfortable to answer, you may choose not to respond. Thank you for becoming part of the story of this space.
